# OpenRamble 0.3.8

## Focused dictation feedback

Recording now uses a compact system-material HUD with a live waveform and
elapsed time, including native Liquid Glass on macOS 26. Processing stays
deliberately quiet, and the HUD disappears as soon as insertion begins. The old
post-insertion speed banner is gone, so your text is the only success
confirmation you see.

## Simpler setup and settings

First-run setup is now three focused steps: introduction, permissions and local
model, then a real test dictation. Settings are reorganized around the shortcut,
behavior, privacy, recognition, model, personal dictionary, updates, and an
informative About page.

## Better technical dictation

OpenRamble now recognizes and formats a broader set of common development terms
without requiring personal dictionary entries. Conservative exact repairs keep
ordinary Russian words from being rewritten as similar-looking product names;
your personal replacements still take priority.

## Accessibility and polish

Glass and standard materials adapt to Reduce Transparency and Increase Contrast,
step transitions respect Reduce Motion, dictionary entries can be added with
Return, and VoiceOver feedback is clearer across recording, permissions, setup,
and recovery states.
