# OpenRamble 0.24.3

This update makes the final handoff after recognition more responsive on a
busy Mac and fixes a dictation-status edge case after errors.

## What changed

- The transcribing HUD no longer runs a continuous symbol animation through
  its Liquid Glass surface. Hidden HUD content also stops rendering, removing
  unnecessary main-thread work after recognition has finished.
- Closing a completed dictation now clears its live HUD state before the panel
  leaves the screen.
- A failure notice remains visible long enough to read, then closes without
  bringing back a stale “Transcribing…” panel. Starting another dictation
  safely supersedes that pending dismissal.

Recognition remains fully local and offline. No dictated text or audio is
added to diagnostics.
