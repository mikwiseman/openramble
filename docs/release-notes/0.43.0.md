# OpenRamble 0.43.0

# Faster on Intel Macs

- On a Mac with an Intel processor, OpenRamble now recognizes speech with its own engine, written in C for the Parakeet model. On the same processor it is about two to three times as fast as before, and it makes as few word errors.
- Taught words, word times in transcribed files and everything else work as they did.
- Macs with Apple silicon are unchanged: they still recognize on the graphics processor.

Your voice and text still never leave your Mac.
