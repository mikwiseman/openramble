# OpenRamble 0.20.0

Choose where OpenRamble appears — and a way to see where a dictation's time
goes.

## Menu bar, Dock, or both

A new setting moves OpenRamble between the menu bar and the Dock, or shows it
in both.

There is no option to hide it entirely, and that is deliberate rather than an
omission. An app you cannot see is an app you cannot open to make visible
again: the setting would be a door that locks behind whoever used it. So this
chooses where OpenRamble appears, never whether. Closing the settings window
no longer takes away a Dock icon you asked to keep, either.

## Where the time goes

Each dictation records how long its steps took — freezing the recording,
preparing the engine, reading the audio, waiting for a turn, and recognising.
A slow dictation now says which step it spent its time in, rather than leaving
you to guess.

Running the numbers over a day of real use says plainly that the speech model
is not the bottleneck: it holds about a fifth of the total and runs some fifty
times faster than real time. Time saved has to come from somewhere else, and
now there is a way to see where.

Nothing you say is ever written to the log. That has not changed.
