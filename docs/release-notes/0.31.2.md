# OpenRamble 0.31.2

- Long dictations, including speech without pauses and recognition retried from history, use bounded audio fragments instead of one large inference call. Speech already recognized during dictation is still reused.
- Stopping a recording and finishing its WAV have more time to complete on a busy Mac. Healthy operations finish immediately; genuine stalls still preserve the local take for recovery.
- Cancelling dictation stops its queued recognition work and prevents late results from appearing in the next dictation.
- Local error reports identify the failed stage and why a recording needed recognition again. Reports still exclude audio, dictated text and error-message contents, and are shared only when you choose.
