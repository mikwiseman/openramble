# OpenRamble 0.36.0

# Long recordings keep every word

- Long recordings, such as imported files and dictations over 30 seconds, are now cut where you pause, the same way the OpenRamble server cuts them. Silence is skipped, and no piece is longer than 20 seconds.
- On our hour-long English test recording, 0.35.0 lost 30 words and 0.36.0 loses none. The hour-long Russian recording and every long test dictation lose none either.
- Recordings that mix Russian and English come out much better. On the hour-long one, errors fell from 39% to 22% of words, and lost words from 2,302 to 1,053.
- English is as accurate as in 0.35.0 or more accurate on every recording we measured. Russian costs a little: 0.65 point more errors on the hour-long recording and 1.7 to 2.5 points more on two long dictations, with no words lost.
- Importing files is faster: five hours of audio took 126 seconds instead of 149.
- The speech engine is now our own build of transcribe.cpp 0.4.1, with one fix for quiet speech in recordings over 30 seconds. On everything OpenRamble sends it, it writes the same text as 0.4.1.

# Recordings

- Recordings no longer lose the last words before a pause. After a pause longer than about 1.4 seconds, the next cut came a second too early, and the words just before it were lost.
- Quiet speech from the other side of a call, such as a call played at low volume, is now transcribed. Before, the other side's speech was skipped if it never got loud. Your microphone works as before.

Your voice and text still never leave your Mac.
