# OpenRamble 0.42.1

# A tidier setup

- The speech model and the permissions are now one list with one layout, in the first-run setup and in Settings → Setup. Each row shows its state, what it is for, and the one button it needs.
- A model download shows its progress bar under the row's own words.
- The download sentence is shorter. It no longer names a mirror and a CDN, and the size is on the button.

# Easier to read

- The smallest gray text is larger everywhere. Sentences are 12 points and times are 11, where some were 10.
- In the Library, your dictations are listed in the same serif they have on the page beside the list. A recording's title and the empty Library's headline use it too.

# Small things

- In the first-run try-out, the note fills the page, and "Skip the try-out" sits beside the key.
- In Settings → About, "Check Now" sits beside the Updates heading.

Your voice and text still never leave your Mac.
