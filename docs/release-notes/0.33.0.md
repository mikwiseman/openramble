# OpenRamble 0.33.0

# Clean output that keeps your meaning

- Hesitations, stutters and restarts disappear on their own: "Um, so we, uh, we should ship it" becomes "So we should ship it". The cleanup only deletes. It never rewords or adds a word, and a word that could be real stays.
- In English, say "Scratch that." to take back the sentence before it, or ", scratch that," to take back what came before it in the sentence. A take that was only a correction inserts nothing, says so, and stays in History.
- On 160 test examples that were set aside while it was built and scored blind, it deleted no real word.
- Meeting transcripts drop hesitations too, but keep "scratch that" and one-word replies, because in a meeting those are things a person said.

# Text that fits where it goes

- In Mail, Notes, Slack, browsers and other writing apps, say "New paragraph." to start one, and a pause of about a second and a half after a sentence starts one too. Terminals and code editors never get a line break you did not ask for.
- In a terminal, end a take with "Send it." as its own sentence to press Return. Said in the middle of a sentence, it stays as text, and no other app ever gets a Return.

# A more accurate model

- Parakeet Ultra replaces Parakeet v3. On all 1,422 English and Russian FLEURS test recordings it had a word error rate of 4.85%, against 5.51% for v3, and it is a 486 MB download instead of 740 MB.
- New installs download Parakeet Ultra. If you already have v3, Settings → Setup offers the upgrade, and nothing downloads until you click. v3 keeps working until Ultra is downloaded, verified and loaded, and then OpenRamble deletes v3.
- Speech recognition now runs on transcribe.cpp 0.4.0.

# Calmer from the first minute

- The welcome screen's button starts the model download, so it runs while you grant permissions. Setup no longer asks you to relaunch, and the window comes back by itself once Accessibility is on.
- The interface follows your Mac's language until you choose one in Settings → General → Interface language.
- The dictation panel keeps one shape from start to finish, and hands-free dictation shows a red lock.
- With VoiceOver on, dictation starts with a short tone instead of a spoken word, so VoiceOver's speech no longer ends up in your text.
- A dictation you star in History now stays however many new ones come after it. Before, five new dictations could push it out.
- Recent Dictations in the menu now shows your History, so it follows the Keep limit, and Delete All also clears Copy Last Dictation.
- Fewer settings. The app follows the system appearance. The model, the permissions and the command-line tool live in a new Setup pane. "Also copy dictations to the clipboard" is gone. To put a dictation on the clipboard, choose Copy Last Dictation in the menu, or set a shortcut for it in Settings → General.
- Recording starts from two actions, Record and Record Screen. Moving or resizing the camera bubble on screen now changes it in the video too.
