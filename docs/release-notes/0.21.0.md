# OpenRamble 0.21.0

Hands-free dictation can finish itself when you stop talking.

## Stopping on silence

A setting, off by default: in hands-free mode, two seconds of quiet ends the
dictation. No more double-pressing to finish.

It applies only to hands-free, and that is the design rather than a limitation.
While you are holding the key you are saying, with your hand, that you have not
finished — a pause for thought is not an ending, and cutting a sentence off
under a held key is what makes voice input feel hostile.

Two more rules worth knowing, because both exist to avoid a specific
frustration. Silence before you have said anything ends nothing, so starting
hands-free in a quiet room does not stop the recording before you begin. And
the quiet clock restarts on your next word, so pausing between sentences keeps
your recording rather than ending it.

## Everything, everywhere, one number

macOS, Windows and Linux now carry the same version. They are one product and
were being counted separately, which had let the desktop packages drift several
releases behind without it being obvious.
