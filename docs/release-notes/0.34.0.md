# OpenRamble 0.34.0

# Fast when your Mac is busy

- After a long pause on a Mac that is short of memory, the speech model now comes back while you are still speaking, so the first dictation is no longer slow. With 8 GB of other apps pressing on memory, text arrived in 0.4 s instead of 0.8 to 0.9 s.
- The microphone no longer drops audio when the Mac is busy. Its audio is now copied on the microphone's own thread, so a busy moment delays it instead of losing it.
- Busy processors barely matter: with every core taken by other work, dictation takes 1.1 to 1.5 times its usual time.

# Clean Verbatim keeps every word

- "We shipped it and, um, it works." now keeps its "and". A hesitation right after a word like "and" or "to" could remove that word too.

# For teams

- OpenRamble can now run on a Linux server: openramble serve turns audio from your own apps into text. It uses every processor core, lets short voice notes go ahead of long meetings, and skips the silence in each person's meeting track. It accepts the same requests as ElevenLabs speech to text. See docs/server.md in the repository.
- A new skill for Codex and Claude Code turns a meeting recording into one page: what was decided, who does what, what is still open, and the full transcript, with every point linked to the moment it was said.

The Mac app still keeps your voice and text on your Mac.
