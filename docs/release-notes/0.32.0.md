# OpenRamble 0.32.0

- Choose English or Russian on the welcome screen or in Settings → General → Language. English is the default, regardless of the Mac's language.
- The interface switches immediately and remembers your choice. Setup, settings, dictation notices, the recordings library, transcript exports and accessibility descriptions are translated. macOS dialogs and system menus use the selected language after the next launch.
- Russian dates, durations and recording controls follow the selected interface language. Recordings, their names and your own text stay unchanged.

Speech recognition, models and privacy settings are unchanged.
