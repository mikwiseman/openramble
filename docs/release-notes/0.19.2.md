# OpenRamble 0.19.2

Every step of a dictation is now timed, so a slow one explains itself.

The record kept for each dictation already listed how long the whole thing
took and how long the speech model worked. Everything between those two was a
single unlabelled lump — and every slow dictation this app has ever had lived
inside it. Finding each one meant reading logs and reasoning backwards.

That lump is now split into named steps: freezing the recording, preparing the
engine, opening and decoding the audio, waiting for a turn, and the
recognition itself. A slow dictation says which of them it spent its time in.

This costs one line per dictation, numbers only, exactly as before. It grows
with how much you talk, not with how hard your Mac is working — logging that
becomes a burden of its own would be a poor way to investigate a performance
problem.

Nothing of what you say is ever written to the log. That has not changed and
will not.
