# OpenRamble 0.33.1

# Every word lands

- The microphone opens faster: it delivers its first audio about 70 ms after you press the key, instead of 250 ms, so more of your first word is kept.
- The speech model stays loaded for an hour after your last dictation or recording, so the next one starts right away. The "Unload model" setting is gone.
- If the microphone does not start within 5 seconds while you hold the key, OpenRamble says so right away instead of waiting for you to let go.
- Dictating during a call no longer records silence. Calls that use Apple's voice processing turned the built-in microphone into three channels that OpenRamble did not hear. The meeting recorder had the same problem and is fixed too.

# A paste knows whether it landed

- Your clipboard comes back as soon as the app reads the dictated text, about a quarter of a second after the paste instead of a full second. A slow app no longer pastes your old clipboard.
- If no app reads the text within 3 seconds, OpenRamble puts your clipboard back, tells you the text was not inserted, and keeps it for Insert Last Dictation.
- Pasting uses the key that types "v" on your keyboard layout, so Dvorak and Turkish F work.
- Pressing the dictation key while a paste is finishing now starts a new take instead of being ignored.

# No take is lost in silence

- A take where no words were recognized is kept in History as "Nothing recognized", with Play and Recognize again. Before, it was deleted with its audio.
- A take that heard only silence names the microphone, so a muted or wrong input is easy to spot.
- Takes with no words have their own History limit, so they never push a dictation out.
- A key pressed by accident after holding for almost a second no longer throws the take away.

Your voice and text still never leave your Mac.
