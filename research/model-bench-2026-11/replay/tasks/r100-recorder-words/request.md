Two things are wrong with recordings.

When I record a meeting, the transcript sometimes misses the last words someone said right before a pause. It happens near the start of a recording and again after longer pauses. The words are in the audio file, just not in the text.

When I record a call, the other person's quiet speech gets no text at all, although I can hear it in the file. My own voice comes through fine.

Find out why and fix both. My microphone must not start picking up the speakers.
